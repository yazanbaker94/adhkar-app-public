# Am I Depressed? - Depression Assessment Quiz

A comprehensive depression assessment tool designed to help individuals understand their mental health status and identify potential contributing factors.

## Features

### Clinical Assessment
- **PHQ-9 Based Questions**: Uses the Patient Health Questionnaire-9, a clinically validated tool for depression screening
- **Comprehensive Evaluation**: Covers mood, interest, sleep, energy, appetite, self-worth, concentration, movement, and suicidal thoughts
- **Duration & Impact Assessment**: Evaluates how long symptoms have been present and their impact on daily life

### Cause Identification
The quiz identifies potential contributing factors to depression:
- **Loneliness/Social Isolation**: Assesses social connections and relationship quality
- **Work-Related Stress**: Evaluates job satisfaction, stress levels, and work-life balance
- **Financial Difficulties**: Measures financial stress and its impact on daily life
- **Relationship Problems**: Assesses romantic and interpersonal relationship satisfaction
- **Health Issues**: Evaluates how health concerns affect mood and functioning
- **Family Problems**: Identifies family-related stressors
- **Academic Pressure**: For students experiencing academic stress
- **Grief/Loss**: For those dealing with bereavement

### Dynamic Question Flow
- **Adaptive Assessment**: Questions adapt based on selected causes
- **One-by-One Presentation**: Questions are presented individually for better focus
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress bar and question counter
- **Navigation**: Previous/Next buttons with validation

### Scoring & Results
- **Weighted Scoring System**: Combines main depression symptoms (70%) with cause-specific factors (30%)
- **Severity Levels**: 
  - Minimal (0-20%)
  - Mild (21-40%)
  - Moderate (41-60%)
  - Severe (61-80%)
  - Extreme (81-100%)
- **Detailed Analysis**: Breakdown of scores by category
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Tailored advice based on results and identified causes

### Privacy & Safety
- **Local Storage Only**: All responses stay on the user's device
- **No Data Collection**: No personal information is stored or transmitted
- **Crisis Resources**: Provides emergency contact information for severe cases
- **Professional Disclaimer**: Clear guidance that this is not a replacement for professional medical advice

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Read the introduction and disclaimer
3. Click "Start Assessment" to begin
4. Answer questions one by one
5. Select potential causes when prompted
6. Answer follow-up questions based on your selections
7. Review your results and recommendations
8. Consider seeking professional help if recommended

## Clinical Notes

This assessment tool is based on established clinical instruments:
- **PHQ-9**: Patient Health Questionnaire-9 for depression screening
- **Clinical Guidelines**: Follows DSM-5 criteria for major depressive disorder
- **Evidence-Based**: Uses validated scoring methods and severity thresholds

## Disclaimer

**Important**: This quiz is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are experiencing severe symptoms or having thoughts of self-harm, please seek immediate professional help or contact emergency services.
